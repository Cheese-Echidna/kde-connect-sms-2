# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KDE desktop users with an Android phone paired through KDE Connect. They use SMS2 for everyday messaging from a desktop and expect contacts, conversations, and recent messages to be ready immediately.

## Product Purpose

SMS2 provides a focused desktop interface for reading and sending phone messages through KDE Connect. Success means reliable synchronization, fast startup from a local cache, correct contact identity and photos sourced only from the paired phone, and messaging behavior that matches a native phone client.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dependable, and familiar. The interface should feel polished and pleasant without drawing attention away from conversations.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental dashboards, excessive cards, novelty interactions, glass effects, heavy animation, loud gradients, or anything that feels more elaborate than a native messaging client.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the conversation as the primary focus.
2. Show changes immediately, then reconcile quietly with the phone.
3. Make offline and synchronization state clear without interrupting normal use.
4. Prefer familiar KDE and messaging conventions over custom interaction patterns.
5. Preserve identity accurately while removing redundant self-participants from one-to-one threads.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow native KDE keyboard and focus conventions, maintain readable contrast, avoid color-only status communication, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
